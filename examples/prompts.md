# Example prompts

## Rates and payments

- What would a $500k place in 60607 cost me monthly with 20% down?
- Compare a 30-year fixed and a 15-year fixed on a $400,000 loan in Denver.
- What do FHA rates look like in Phoenix for a 640 credit score?

## Lenders

- Who are the top VA lenders in Austin?
- Which credit unions close the most loans in the Chicago metro?
- Look up a lender by its NMLS ID and show its approval and pull-through rates.

## The place

- Do I need flood insurance in 33139?
- How do home prices in Boise compare to the rest of the country?
- What hazards should I check before buying in Paradise, California?

## Treasury

- What is the current 10-year Treasury yielding, and how has it moved this year?
- Price the on-the-run 2-year note at a 4% yield.
- What is a $1,000 Series EE bond bought in 2005 worth today?
